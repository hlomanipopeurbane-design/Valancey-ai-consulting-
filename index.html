Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Information you choose to provide

The enquiry form may include your name, business name, location, email address, chosen services, budget range, duration and project description. Please do not include passwords, payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive information.

2. How the form works

This static website has no database and does not itself store or submit your form details to a server. When you submit the form, your browser opens WhatsApp with the details pre-filled in a message addressed to Valence Agency. You can review, edit or cancel the message. The information is sent only if you choose to press Send.

3. WhatsApp and third-party services

WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its own terms and privacy policy apply when you use it. Valence Agency cannot control how third-party services process data. Avoid including confidential or sensitive information in your enquiry.

4. Purpose and retention

Information you send is intended to respond to your enquiry, discuss scope and prepare a proposal. Messages may remain in WhatsApp chats and backups according to the settings of the people using those services. You can ask Valence Agency to stop contacting you or to delete information it controls, subject to applicable obligations.

5. Sharing and security

Valence Agency does not sell enquiry information. Information may be shared with a service provider only where needed to assess or deliver work, with appropriate care and where lawful. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and contact

Submitting the form is optional. You can contact us through WhatsApp to ask about your information or withdraw from further contact.
